Featured Project — Baltimore 311 Civic Data Analytics

Project Overview

The Baltimore 311 Civic Data Analytics project explores the application of data science, statistical analysis, and artificial intelligence to municipal service request data.

The project focuses on understanding citizen service requests, identifying operational patterns, investigating potential service delivery inefficiencies, and exploring opportunities for data-driven government improvement.

Project Objectives

Analyze municipal service request data.

Identify patterns and trends in citizen service requests.

Investigate service request frequency and geographic distribution.

Explore service response and resolution times.

Identify potential operational inefficiencies.

Develop meaningful data visualizations.

Explore predictive analytics for municipal service demand.

Investigate opportunities for business process improvement and automation.

Support evidence-based government decision-making.

Proposed Data Analytics Methodology

Data Acquisition

Collect publicly available Baltimore 311 municipal service request data.

Data Cleaning and Preparation

Identify missing values, duplicate records, inconsistent formatting, and incomplete information.

Data Organization

Structure and categorize municipal service request information for efficient analysis.

Exploratory Data Analysis

Investigate service request patterns, frequency distributions, temporal trends, geographic concentrations, and relationships between variables.

Statistical Analysis

Apply descriptive and inferential statistical methods to investigate municipal service patterns and operational performance.

Data Visualization

Develop charts, dashboards, geographic visualizations, and analytical reports to communicate findings.
